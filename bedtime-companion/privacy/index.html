Bedtime Companion

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Bedtime Companion is a Chrome extension that helps you stop browsing at your chosen bedtime. This page explains what happens with your information. The short version: nothing leaves your device.

What we collect

Nothing. Bedtime Companion does not collect, sell or share any personal information or browsing data, and it has no servers to send anything to.

What stays on your device

Your settings (bedtime, wake-up time, active days, snooze length and on/off preferences) and tonight's progress (such as snoozes used) are saved with Chrome's built-in extension storage. If you use Chrome Sync, Chrome may sync your settings between your own devices. We never see this data.

Website access

The extension needs access to the websites you visit only to show its own reminder and bedtime screens on top of them. It does not read, record or change the content of those pages.

No tracking

No analytics, no advertising, no cookies, and no network requests to any server.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
